# Product Vision — TabLusa

## Problem
Guitarists who want to learn Portuguese songs struggle to find good chord sheets and
tabs. Existing platforms (Cifra Club, Ultimate Guitar) have limited or inconsistent
coverage of Portuguese music, and their experience is not built around this audience.

## Target user (primary)
A guitarist who plays with friends and wants to learn new Portuguese songs. Mostly plays
chords over lyrics, often reading from a phone on a table or stand while playing.

## Value proposition
1. **Catalogue focused on Portuguese music**, curated for quality.
2. **A playing-first experience** for chord sheets and tabs: readable on mobile while
   playing, with tools such as transposition, capo, chord diagrams and auto-scroll.
3. **Close relationship with users**: users request songs and are notified when the
   request is fulfilled.
4. **Portuguese identity** in the visual design (branding, not the core value).

## Content model
Hybrid: the community submits chord sheets/tabs and fulfils requests; submissions are
reviewed by a moderator (initially the author) before publishing. Requests can be voted
on to prioritise them. See decision 002.

## Hypotheses to validate
- Coverage of Portuguese music on existing platforms is poor enough to motivate users
  to switch (check: sample of 20 Portuguese songs on Cifra Club / Ultimate Guitar).
- Users will submit content, not only consume it.

## Non-goals (Phase 1)
- Public launch with copyrighted content (see decision 001).
- Monetisation.
- Native mobile apps (responsive web only).
- Portuguese guitar (guitarra portuguesa) notation — possible future niche.
